# E019: historical threshold-data extension

The user authorized extending/improving data after the representation-first trial exposed sparse threshold evidence. This work adds a separate, reproducible original-source overlay rather than modifying the frozen database or training snapshots. It does not fit another model or change the adopted forecast/69.86% turnout.

Read the frozen inclusion protocol and factual source audit under `data/snapshots/threshold-data-2026-10-25/`. Discovery covers the final28 publication days in each of the six campaigns, starting from audited sources and original-publisher links. This is a convenience extension, not a random or exhaustive census. Preserve every reviewed exact percentage in an admitted release, whether its list ultimately passed or failed. Secondary sources, unresolved graphics, hypothetical lineups, post-election data and unverified PDF leads remain separate/excluded.

Eight new original releases have complete120-seat tables; two existing releases gain percentages. The overlay expands24to32releases and20to37mapped exact percentage observations across five campaigns. Seven of the37observations concern eventual winners, but these repeat only three election/list outcomes across two campaigns. The old exact-percentage subset had no eventual-pass example. Raw numbers alone are not37independent calibration outcomes.

Publication, fieldwork, known revision dates, sample size, invited count, method, undecided context and source links are preserved. Export publication-horizon bands1–7/8–14/15–28 and the original T−1 effective-age eligibility independently. March7,2021N12 was fielded March4; do not backdate its publication. N12/Walla share Midgam; MakoMorningApril2019uses PanelsPolitics. Publisher is not firm identity.

All percentages remain **as published**. Their exact denominator and undecided allocation are unverified; there is no normalization to valid votes, no reconstruction of vote percentages from seats and no invented numerical bounds for missing shares. A below-threshold percentage is not automatically a calibrated event probability, nor is a positive seat projection a measured raw percentage. Historical first-release archive vintages remain unverified. All these limits are structured export fields.

Run `python scripts/prepare_threshold_data.py` with standard-library Python3.12. It validates dates, identity, complete seat totals, duplicate URL/ID guards and conflicting enrichment values, preserves frozen inputs, and exports all observations with outcome labels plus a separate pre-election predictor whitelist. Reports include source release JSON, CSV, missing/unmapped records, input hashes and a coverage figure. The dedicated hosted workflow tests gates and repeats/reference-compares all outputs byte-for-byte. Raw publisher pages/images are not redistributed, and unavailable raw-response hashes are not invented.

This collection makes a later reported-share model test more meaningful, but does not itself establish predictive improvement or certify a full vote-based allocator. Any later fit must be chronological, preserve shared campaign/firm/outcome dependence, handle informative missingness and compare source-matched horizons. PR execution evidence is authoritative; local Git metadata remains stale. No source promotion into the adopted model, merge or submission.
